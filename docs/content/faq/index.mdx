## General

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Can I migrate data from another authentication plugin?">
    Identica does not currently ship official migrators for other authentication plugins.

    If you want to migrate anyway, ask in [Discord](https://discord.arcadeya.com).
    We may be able to provide a temporary Python migration script that you can review and run locally against your own data.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Flow

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from moving or breaking blocks during the Identica flow?">
    Identica is an identity plugin.
    It does not handle movement restrictions, block interaction, or general gameplay isolation during registration, authentication, or migration.

    Use a separate environment that blocks those actions while the player is in the Identica flow.
    We recommend [PicoLimbo](https://picolimbo.quozul.dev/about/introduction.html), because it can host virtual servers inside your proxy and does not require separate full backend servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from using commands during the Identica flow?">
    This is already warned about in the installation guides.
    You should block commands on your proxy, or use a community addon for that purpose.

    [Identica CommandBlocker](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-commandblocker) can automatically block unrelated commands and allow only the ones needed during the Identica flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop players from seeing announcements, staff chat, or other messages during the Identica flow?">
    That should usually be handled outside Identica itself by the rest of your network setup.

    You can configure your other plugins so those messages are only sent to the servers where they belong, or use a community addon such as [Identica ChatBlocker](https://modrinth.com/plugin/identica-chatblocker).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
